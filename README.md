# Grow Club · CRM prototype for Gini & Jony

A student concept prototype by Guhan (MBA Fashion & Lifestyle Business Management, Pearl Academy Bengaluru) for a CRM capstone on Gini & Jony. **Not an official Gini & Jony product, app or store.** All families, stores and figures are illustrative test data.

## What's here
- `/` — Grow Club platform: parent app, staff tablet, HQ CRM console, franchise view, live demo and a self-running guided demo
- `/store/` — 3D store model of the CRM flows on a Bengaluru high street
- `media/Grow_Club_guided_demo.mp4` — 2-minute screen recording of the guided demo
- `media/Grow_Club_CRM_initiatives.mp4` — 2:47 motion video walking through every Grow Club CRM initiative from the capstone deck

Static HTML, no build step. Libraries load from cdnjs and jsDelivr; fonts from Google Fonts.
